What to expect

You'll learn basic steps, how to dance with a partner, and how to move to swing music – all at a comfortable pace in a supportive, friendly environment.

At Swing Shift, we teach authentic Lindy Hop passed down from the legends we learned from, like Frankie Manning and Norma Miller. It's proper swing dancing for people who want to learn the real thing: expressive, rhythmic, and tons of fun.

In our classes you'll master foundational techniques, classic moves, and solo jazz – all danced to genuine swing-era music from the 1930s and 40s.

An afternoon of dancing to swinging jazz, with DJ TK Mixx and guests.

Only just started? Come anyway. We know a social dance sounds mildly terrifying when you've had three classes. It isn't. Everyone in the room was a beginner once, and an afternoon is a far gentler way in than a late night: try a few things out, meet people, get your ear in for the music.

Bring a bottle of water and a change of clothes — it gets warm. There are hangers for coats but no cloakroom, so pack tidy. Closest car park is Avenue Road, plus some on-street parking nearby; check the signs.

There's a bar at the venue where you can get tea, coffee or something stronger – please don't bring any outside drinks besides water. Contributions to the snack table are very welcome. There's no kitchen at the venue, so it needs to be eat-by-hand, no-plate food.

Why the name? Well it's the nautical word for the water barrel where you hang about and gossip. It's also an Artie Shaw tune. And it's roughly what you do on a dancefloor.

    Teachers

    Tom Kerwin

    Dancing since

    Herrång Dance Camp, UK & Europe
    DJ name
    TK Mixx

    Tom has been dancing Lindy Hop since 1999, starting with a few classes as a student in Cambridge. But he really got bitten by the bug when he first moved to London and stumbled into the LSE Swing Dance Society. From there, he started studying with dance legends Ronnie Slide and Angela Andrew, and became obsessed with this deep, rich art form.

    Before long Tom was teaching with LSE 'Swingers' founder Dawn Wong. Later, his partnership with Angela helped him develop his approach to teaching, finding balance between technique and spirit. In the intervening years, he's taught at dance events around the UK and Europe, including the internationally-renowned Herrang Dance Camp in Sweden.

    Tom brings creativity and musicality to his dancing, and loves sharing the music and the dance with others, breaking down techniques and inspiring people to find their own style.

    He has also been known to DJ swing dance events in London, Stockholm and beyond (his extremely cool DJ name is TK Mixx).

    Corissa Nunn

    One left, one right (confirmed)

    Corissa started dancing Lindy Hop in 2016 and stepped into a teaching role in 2023. She loves the way Lindy Hop gives you the power to have an unspoken conversation with another person – and helps you discover your own presence.

    "If you keep telling yourself you'll never be a dancer, I know EXACTLY how you feel," she says. Diagnosed with dyspraxia in childhood, Corissa always saw herself as having two left feet. But having learned the ropes from Tom (who is far too modest to admit how good he is), she's glad to confirm that she does in fact have a left foot AND a right foot, and they both bloomin' love to do Lindy Hop.

    These days, nothing makes her happier than working with others to develop their skills and confidence in this outrageously rewarding dance.

    Q&A

    Still have questions?

    We're here to help! Drop us a message and we'll get right back to you.

    Email us Message on Instagram
    Are brand-new beginners welcome?

    Absolutely! Our Tuesday beginner classes in Talbot Heath are designed for people who have never danced Lindy Hop before. Space is limited, so email us at team@swingshiftlindyhop.com to book.

    Do I need to come with a partner?

    Nope! You'll rotate partners throughout the class, so everyone gets to dance with everyone.

    Is Lindy Hop hard to learn?

    Well... it's not the easiest dance in the world. The good news is that we've got decades of experience getting people started, getting comfortable and having fun. And obviously we're biased, but it's oh-so-worth-it.

    What should I bring to class?

    Bring a bottle of water and yourself. Some people like to bring a spare t-shirt and deodorant in case you get sweaty. You can buy proper drinks at the venue: tea, coffee, soft drinks and alcohol, so please avoid bringing in anything other than water.

    What should I wear?

    Comfortable clothes you can move freely in – think casual wear, even relaxed gym wear. Trainers or flat shoes work well, avoid high heels or heavy boots.

    Are there social and fitness aspects too?

    There sure are. Put it this way... we don't bother going to the gym ;) and for many folks the Lindy Hop community becomes a source of lifelong friendships.

    Where can I park?

    BOH Studios is located near the Triangle in Bournemouth. There are car parks very nearby, and some free on-street parking if you'd rather walk a little way.

    Do I have to buy a package to join in?

    Nope, it's pay as you go. Intermediates can just show up and pay on the door. Beginners pay on the night too, but need to book each class in advance.

    Will I learn fixed patterns of moves in your classes?

    Sometimes, but we like to mix it up.

    We do teach short sequences as well as longer routines (including classics such as California, Stops, Lindy Chorus, Shim Sham, Tranky Doo, Trickeration, Big Apple, ...). But often our classes are more conceptual, where we share an idea for you to explore and incorporate into your social dancing. The latter is a bit like Marmite... some people love this approach, some don't! We're always open to honest feedback, so please do let us know how you found it afterwards.

    Do you have a performance troupe?

    We do indeed. It's invite only, with a monthly subscription. If you're interested in really challenging yourself with advanced techniques, and want to supercharge your skills at breakneck tempos, come chat to us about working towards it.
